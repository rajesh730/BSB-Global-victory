# Page Inventory and Visual Acceptance Criteria

### Homepage `/`
- Navy brand hero, globe illustration, small journal card; sections for principles, three country cards, four-step process, featured services, resources and FAQs.
- CTA first in hero and last as full-width band.

### Study abroad `/study-abroad`
- Dedicated editorial page hero, three-card destination grid with licensed photos, practical selection checklist.

### Country guides `/study-abroad/*`
- Three unique page titles and licensed photos, considerations, checklist, official-government source panel, common-question block and links to the other destinations.
- Accessible external official links open new tabs with safe rel values.

### Services `/services`
- Six designed service cards in responsive grid; transparency information on service scope/fees.

### Test preparation `/test-preparation`
- Three visually distinct test cards, four-stage preparation process and a clear note that schedules are not confirmed.

### About `/about`
- Editorial value proposition, four principle cards, transparent placeholder for actual people and credentials.

### Resources `/resources`
- Four original resources with categories, reading time and live routes.

### Article `/resources/*`
- Four complete articles with section headings, sticky table of contents, related links and a clear legal-information note.

### Contact `/contact`
- Split layout with verified public contact config, accessible form, draft-only submission mechanics, privacy notice and preparation guidance.

### Privacy / Terms `/privacy`, `/terms`
- Well-scanned legal-document layout, draft disclosures and no invented legal entity.

### Missing route
- Friendly branded 404 that returns visitors to the homepage.

Total renderable routes from the current content dataset: **20** (one homepage + 8 additional fixed informational pages + 6 country guides + 4 articles + branded 404). Count 404 separately from normal 19 URLs.
