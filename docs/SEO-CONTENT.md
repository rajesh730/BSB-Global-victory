# SEO, Information Architecture & Marketing Handoff

## Navigation

Seven clear top-level links plus a single action CTA:

1. Home
2. Study abroad (country submenu)
3. Our services
4. Test preparation
5. About us
6. Resources
7. Contact

Global CTA: **Book counselling**. The contact form currently creates a copyable/email enquiry draft until a secure production channel is implemented.

## Internal linking rules

- Homepage links to every destination, key service section, the first three articles and Contact.
- Destinations hub links to all three country guides and to the course-selection resource.
- Each country guide links up to the destination hub, Contact, the document guide, three alternate countries and an official government source.
- Resources index links to every guide. Guides have a table of contents, links to two related articles and a route to Contact.
- Footer links to the destinations, services, resources, About, Contact and both legal notices.
- Use descriptive anchor text such as “Application document guide” instead of generic “Click here”.

## Priority search intent

Search intent should guide the topic, not lead to keyword stuffing. Initial themes:

| Page | Primary theme | Supporting intent |
|---|---|---|
| Home | Education consultancy in Nepal | Study abroad guidance, student counselling |
| Destination guides | Study in Japan/UK/South Korea from Nepal | Requirements, courses, costs, planning |
| Services | Education consultancy services | Applications, counselling, university shortlisting |
| Test prep | IELTS and PTE guidance in Nepal | Choosing an accepted English-language test |
| Resources | Study abroad guides for Nepali students | Document preparation, budget, course selection |
| Contact | BSB Global contact | Counselling enquiry, consultant contact |

For Kathmandu or other city-level pages, only create location content where BSB Global has a genuine physical office or credible local service presence. Avoid duplicate location doorway pages.

## Built-in technical SEO

- Per-route titles, descriptions, OG metadata and responsive markup.
- Automatically generated Open Graph image.
- `generateStaticParams` for country guides and articles.
- Canonical paths after a verified live URL is configured.
- XML sitemap with all published static and dynamic content when verified.
- `robots.txt` and metadata remain noindex/disallow before business verification.
- Organization JSON-LD only once public launch is approved. No fake reviews, FAQ rich-result spam or fabricated awards.
- Semantic headings, meaningful links, original text and next/image-compatible local art assets.

## Content governance

Every content update must record: author, reviewer, date, source, change summary and next review date (your CMS or editorial log can hold these outside the initial repo).

- **Student visa / work rights / financial thresholds:** Check authoritative government source before publication, after major policy change, and at least quarterly.
- **Course entry criteria / tuition / language tests:** Verify against individual institution before publishing a definitive claim.
- **Service fees, branches, counsellors, licence:** Approve with the actual business responsible person before displaying.
- **Student testimonials:** Obtain written, revocable permission and do not manufacture outcomes.
- **External country links:** Check quarterly for redirects and broken links.
- **Editorial guides:** Review at least every 6 months or sooner after policy changes.

## Analytics and conversion (not installed)

A future privacy-approved setup might track:

- `contact_view` (contact page visit)
- `enquiry_draft_prepared` (form prepared/copied; **not** a lead submission)
- `enquiry_received` (only after a real server confirms a received enquiry)
- `telephone_click` (click-to-call, when a verified number is present)
- `official_source_outbound` (official immigration authority click)
- `destination_guide_view` (which destination content is helpful)

Respect local privacy law and consent requirements before adding cookies, advertising tags or analytics. Do not report copy-to-clipboard or mailto link clicks as successful form conversions.

## Practical campaigns

- Publish original Nepali-student-focused guides: application timelines, budget planning, how to compare institutions, explaining qualification levels.
- Integrate with a genuine Google Business Profile only for a verified, eligible office and consistent NAP data.
- Use Search Console to identify pages earning impressions and improve answers to actual user questions.
- Run ads only once the verified service scope, responsive landing pages, enquiry tracking and privacy notices are live.
- Keep digital marketing claims conservative: no “100% visa guarantee” or invented approval statistics.
